# Retained goal suppliers

Use `fm_report_retained_goal_suppliers` with a canonical scorer, his team and the exact selected result references. The reader checks the fixture sides, the full selected-team roster, canonical player identities and individual goal/assist counters. The selected team's player goals must add up to its stored final score. A result affected by opposing own goals may therefore have unavailable bounds. The response identifies each covered fixture. A missing selection cannot count as a zero.

The calculation permits one credited assist per goal or an unassisted goal and excludes self-assists. It reports the minimum and maximum number of assists each teammate could have supplied to the selected scorer. If the two values agree, every possible allocation of the team's recorded goals and assists gives that count. The extrema for different teammates need not occur together. Whole-selection bounds sum independent fixture ranges and identify uncovered fixtures.

A separate projection reads the native GoalscorersString timeline from result +0x78. On supported type-0 fixtures it reconciles both PERSON references with the named roster, the credited score and every player's goal/assist total. The native accessor resolves the primary PERSON at +0 and the official Player2 property at +4. Signed -1 denotes a missing secondary PERSON. It preserves Player2 by that property name. The secondary reference's individual assist role has yet to be proved for this collection. Unsupported event subtypes or mismatched sources withhold the timeline independently of the counter-based bounds.

The Lille, Southampton, Arsenal, Chelsea, Olympiacos and Everton selected results from the open Newcastle career supplied 243 player rows and 20 goal records in the November 6 control. The independent type-0 corpus reconciled 244 goal records to 3,364 player rows across 83 distinct earlier live captures. Counter agreement checks data coverage. It cannot distinguish a swap of two providers between two goals.
