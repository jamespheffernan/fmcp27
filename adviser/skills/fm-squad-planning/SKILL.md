# Squad construction

## Establish the football requirements

Read `fm-tools` and `fm-live-investigation` through `fm_read_skill`. Bind the running career, date and club. Load `fm-tactical-analysis` when the intended style or phase roles need establishing. Translate the user's game model into requirements for each unit: progression, creation, box occupation, defensive cover and pressing. Complete when the roles to fill and the planning horizon are explicit.

## Account for the existing squad

Use the club's squad groups for a club-wide plan; distinguish the Senior roster, youth teams and away loans. Preserve duplicated memberships, unresolved identities and unavailable profiles. For serious options, read current club, contract club, loan state, positions, relevant attributes, stored readiness and available contract terms. Treat position familiarity as evidence for a trial, not automatic eligibility. One versatile player cannot provide simultaneous cover in two selected positions.

Separate immediate cover, a developing successor and a proposed recruit. Compare the relevant strengths and weaknesses for the intended role rather than ranking everyone by CA/PA. Current tools expose unmasked values; a request for a manager-known view remains unsupported until a source-bound knowledge policy exists. Stored youth potential is not a development guarantee or a scout's perceived stars.

For playing-time concerns or rotation priorities, load `fm-playing-time-dynamics` to compare agreements with the supported usage window and build a conditional minutes plan. It preserves current competition counters, retained appearances and historical-start unknowns separately from happiness and promises. Complete when every recommended departure or addition has a named replacement path and every retained player has a plausible role.

## Compare ways to fill the gap

Consider role changes, internal promotion, a loan, a permanent signing and retaining the current player. For promotion or a development loan, load `fm-training-development` to connect the intended role and minutes to a reviewable pathway. Use supported team training calendars, staff attributes and assessments when relevant. A schedule or staff rating alone does not prove training effectiveness, individual workload, morale or an agreed pathway.

For external candidates, follow the live investigation's complete search and pagination rules. Separate playing merit, stored joining-interest assessments, seller price and contract demands. An absent assessment leaves interest unknown. Load `fm-contracts-loans` for contract and loan terms, payers, wage contributions, expiry and supported options; it preserves stored conditions separately from activation.

Load `fm-club-finance` before recommending a financial commitment. Test combined fees and wages, and state unpriced obligations. Retain fixture-specific bans, registration, medical clearance and other unknown eligibility gates. Complete when the preferred option has a football benefit, a cost or unresolved cost, and a credible alternative.

## Give a prioritized plan

Lead with the most consequential role gap, the evidence that makes it urgent and the action to take. Explain what could change the priority: an expiring contract, unavailable cover, recruitment cost or lack of minutes for a successor. Label the club facts, calculations and football judgments separately. End with the next decision the evidence supports; preserve unknown board objectives, promises and dressing-room dynamics.
