What to change

After half-time Sule and Holm both sat below the ball (average 39% up the pitch against 47% before the break), which left Varela alone between the lines and invited Rovers' press. Their goal came from that spell.

  • Keep Holm as a box-to-box midfielder in the second phase; only Sule holds.
  • The left channel (Reid to Ferreira, 18 passes) produced 0.71 xG; repeat it against Calderbrook, whose right side is weaker.
  • Ward completed 6 of 14 crosses but none led to a shot. Ask for cut-backs instead.
