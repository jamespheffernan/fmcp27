How to beat them

  • Press Pereira on receipt: 19 passes a match come from Kowalczyk to him, the most of any pair.
  • Our right-back stays narrow; the right winger tracks Pereira's overlap rather than Okonjo.
  • Their pivot sits deep (average 40–41% up the pitch). Early diagonals behind Whitfield have produced 0.4 xG a match against them.
  • Delacroix touches the ball 29 times a match; deny Varga the half-turn and the striker is isolated.
