What the role needs

The 4-2-3-1 asks the left-back to tuck into midfield in possession and recover the flank when the ball is lost. That puts pace, stamina and positioning first, with passing and composure close behind.

  • Recovery: Pace and Acceleration of 14 or more.
  • Inverted build-up: Passing, Composure and Decisions of 12 or more.
  • Availability: under 23, contract within reach, no reported interest from a bigger club.
