What’s possible?

Six questions asked on one Newcastle career, from a half-time read to a plan for the season. The answers are real, shortened to fit.

MAT-1

Tactical adjustment

Please check out my active match against PSG. We’re just past half-time and I want your tactical analysis.

Reading the match

Keep your attacking shape. Add protection rather than chase the game.

Change Shea Charles from Box-to-Box Midfielder to Defensive Midfielder. Keep Positive mentality and the Mid Block. You lose a supporting runner.

Check again at 55 to 60 minutes: you want your box chances to continue and Haaland to receive less in the middle.

OPP-1

Opposition scouting

We’re away at Man United on the 18th. How will they line up, and who do I need to stop?

Reading their last six matches

Expect a 4-2-3-1 with Højlund up front unless Šeško is fit. Cut off Amad’s supply first, without dragging a centre-back out to chase him.

Stay compact through the middle, make their wide players beat two defenders, and don’t send both full-backs forward together.

MAT-3

Diagnosing a defensive weakness

They just had a goal disallowed that came from the same move all their chances are coming from: a big switch from one winger to the other, wide open, then a pullback cross to Haaland or a finish.

Reading both sides’ live tactics

That’s the warning to act now. What you describe is missing cover on the far side, not a failure to mark Haaland.

Without the ball: both full-backs to Holding Full-Back. They stay connected to the back line instead of stepping out, so the far-side defender starts goal-side of the switch. The cost: PSG’s wide build-up gets more time.

With the ball: both wing-backs to Full-Back. Less ground to recover when you lose it. The cost: fewer overlaps.

Keep Charles holding and the Mid Block. Don’t press harder or pull a centre-back out to the winger: that opens exactly the pullback to Haaland.

Watch PSG’s next two or three attacks: the winger they switch to should receive with your full-back already goal-side.

MAT-2

Player performance analysis

Woltemade and Henrique are both having terrible matches according to their ratings. I would love to know what specifically they’re not doing well.

Reading the match

They’re struggling in different ways: Woltemade has been starved of service and has now missed a major chance; Henrique has received plenty but produced little direct threat.

Woltemade: isolation, and now a costly miss. He has seen little of the ball, and his one shot, from near the penalty spot, missed the target. Henrique: involvement without penetration. 30 of the passes he received came from Livramento, but only two of his own reached Woltemade.

CAP-1

Squad planning

We’re still in four competitions. If we go deep in all of them, do I have enough cover in every position, and can I keep the playing-time promises I’ve made?

Modelling 37 matches, minutes and promises

You have enough players for four competitions, but left-back is the pressure point. Give Robinson minutes now and complete Miley’s loan.

Deep runs mean 37 more matches, including 11 in 28 days. Give Robinson the left side before the deadline, taking minutes from Ramsey and Gittens rather than adding late cameos. Diouf’s promise is on track.

SCT-2

Transfer search

Find me a right-footed centre-back who’s a defensive beast. Search the whole database.

Searching every player in the save

De Ligt is your strongest fit. Bremer is the short-term option worth investigating.

De Ligt: the clearest physical and defensive upgrade on Charles. Strength 18, bravery 18, marking and positioning 17. Age and cost are the drawbacks.

Bremer: a short-term option with a contract expiring in June. Strong tackling and heading, but pace 13 makes him less suited to defending huge spaces.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Install.

    Download FMCP 27, run its installer and paste one line into Steam. It works with the Steam copy of Football Manager 27.

    INS-1

    Set up once

    Steam on Mac
    1. 1

      Download FMCP 27 for Mac and unzip it in your Downloads folder.

    2. 2

      Open Terminal and paste this line. It installs FMCP 27 to the folder FMCP27 in your home folder and asks for no password.

      sh ~/Downloads/FMCP27/install.sh
    3. 3

      In Steam, open Football Manager 27 → Properties → General → Launch Options and paste the line the installer showed. It is on your clipboard already, and has your own user name in it:

      /Users/your-name/FMCP27/fm_launch.sh %command%
    4. 4

      Add FMCP 27 to your assistant. The installer ends by showing the line for Claude, for Codex and for Pi.

    5. 5

      Start Football Manager 27 from Steam as usual and load your save.

    What macOS asks

    Nothing, if you install from Terminal as above. FMCP 27’s programs are signed by Turf Terrace Ltd and notarized by Apple.

    To remove it

    Delete the line in Steam, then delete the FMCP27 folder. FMCP 27 changes nothing in the game’s files or in Steam.
